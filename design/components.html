Overlays

Use a dialog to confirm, a drawer to edit a record without losing your place in the list, a menu for secondary actions, and ⌘K to jump anywhere.

Charts

2px lines, a ~10% area wash, 2px surface gaps between donut segments and heatmap cells, and solid hairline grids. Every chart has a hover/keyboard tooltip and a hidden data table. The categorical order (green, blue, orange, violet) is validated for colour-vision deficiency in both themes.
